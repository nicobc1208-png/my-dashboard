# Weekly Review

Run my weekly life review. Pull context from available sources and give a structured assessment.

Steps:
1. Check Google Calendar (`mcp__Google_Calendar__list_events`) for the past 7 days — summarize what actually happened
2. Check Gmail (`mcp__Gmail__search_threads`) for anything important/unresolved from this week
3. Ask me to paste or describe: goals completed, workouts done, books read, finances changed

Then output a structured review:

## Week of [date]

**Wins** — 3 bullets max, specific
**Misses** — what didn't happen that was planned
**Health snapshot** — anything notable (workouts, streaks, nutrition)
**Finance pulse** — any notable movements
**Next week focus** — 1-3 priorities, not a wish list

Keep it honest and direct. Skip categories with nothing to say.
